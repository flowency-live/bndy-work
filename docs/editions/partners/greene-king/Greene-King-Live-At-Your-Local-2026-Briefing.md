The largest unresolved gap is the occupied leased, tenanted and franchise estate.

## What the programme is

Live At Your Local is Greene King’s nationwide grassroots music campaign. Untapped is the emerging-artist competition within it. The wider programme includes ordinary pub music events, so a Live At Your Local listing does not automatically identify a competition heat. The campaign’s current landing page confirms that the summer programme has wrapped while directing customers to continuing local music. [Official campaign page](https://www.greeneking.co.uk/live-at-your-local)

## Marketing, partners and associated activity

Danny Jones was the campaign ambassador and appeared in its launch film. Greene King’s 28 July release positioned affordable, shared live music against solitary listening and expensive major concerts. Its commissioned survey of 2,000 adults reported that 62% of listening happened alone, 64% considered major concerts financially inaccessible, and 27% were seeking cheaper entertainment. These are campaign research claims, not independently measured results of the programme. [Launch announcement](https://www.greeneking.co.uk/newsroom/danny-jones-backs-the-return-of-greene-kings-live-at-your-local)

A linked Jack Daniel’s & Coca-Cola promotion involved Brown-Forman, Coca-Cola Europacific Partners and Greene King. Its terms specified a 10p donation per qualifying serve sold in Greene King and Belhaven pubs from 19 July to 14 September 2026, capped at £20,000, to Music Venue Trust. That cap is not evidence that £20,000 was actually raised. A separate app game ran from 20 July to 14 September, offering Wembley tickets and drink prizes. Its terms are separate from the artist competition. [Promotion and donation terms](https://www.greeneking.co.uk/pubs/greater-london/rising-sun/live-at-your-local)

**Do not add these three datasets together.** They overlap. Exact URL uniqueness does not eliminate duplicate physical venues across brands. Same-name pubs have not been merged, and historical addresses have not been silently attached to current records using uncertain name matches.

### Why this cannot honestly be called every owned pub

Greene King distinguishes managed pubs from its leased, tenanted and franchise businesses. Its August 2026 statement reported 97% Pub Partners occupancy and said only a handful of pubs were available to run at a time. A recruitment directory therefore cannot enumerate the occupied estate. [Official Pub Partners update](https://www.greeneking.co.uk/newsroom/record-high-applications)

Public corporate pages also disagree on the headline estate size: the [company page](https://www.greeneking.co.uk/our-company) says 2,700, while the [pubs, restaurants and hotels page](https://www.greeneking.co.uk/pubs-restaurants-hotels) says 2,600. Neither supplies a complete dated property register that reconciles to these lists. A coverage percentage would imply precision the sources do not support.

Other limits are that standalone Hickory’s and Everly locations, and separate hotel inventories, have not been fully enumerated. A brand affiliation does not prove the group owns the freehold. Pub names, brands, operators and ownership can change independently. The workbook is consequently a prospecting and research inventory, with a clear route back to its sources.

## What remains unverified and what to request

The researched public material did not establish the full 2026 venue count, all regional winners and finalists, every artist and performance time, campaign budget, artist fees, booking agency, event-feed availability, or confirmed 2027 programme. It also did not resolve every pub’s legal ownership.

For a usable complete estate file, request site ID, trading name and aliases, full address, postcode, coordinates, brand, operating model, trading status, official URL and the file’s effective date. If legal property ownership matters, request the owning entity and tenure as separate fields.

For music listings or a commercial integration, request event IDs linked to site IDs, local dates and times, artists, event type, admission details, cancellation status, source URLs and image-reuse permissions. The existing public schedule supplies venue leads, but it is missing the information needed for reliable consumer event listings.

The most useful next commercial discussion would concern the 2027 programme and year-round pub music: who controls the schedule, whether the group can provide an authorised feed, and which participating sites can be included. This is a recommendation based on the evidence above, not a confirmed Greene King partnership offering.
